About Mama Qadeer Memorial Library

Mama Qadeer Memorial Library is a student-focused initiative associated with Baloch Students Council University of Lahore.


The purpose of this initiative is to encourage reading, learning and knowledge sharing among students and to make educational resources easier to access.
